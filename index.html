Learning Journey exemplars

Current PRISM version: Beta 0.9

This corpus collects completed PRISM Learning Journeys so lecturers, learning designers, and educational technologists can study how a teaching need was turned into a coherent sequence of learner activities.

Each entry emphasises the design behind the journey—the teaching need, the capability it develops, the patterns at work, and a principle you can transfer—then offers a direct launch into the published experience and a prompt you can adapt for your own brief.

About these exemplars

These resources were created using PRISM at different stages of its development. They illustrate different approaches to self-directed learning, but may not reflect the latest features, templates or capabilities of the current version. An exemplar’s creation version may therefore differ from the current PRISM version shown above.

The corpus is a collection of examples rather than a continuously updated showcase. Exemplars may be refreshed when there is a significant reason to do so, but are not routinely regenerated following every PRISM update.
