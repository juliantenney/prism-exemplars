# Adaptation consultation prompt — The Decision That Went Wrong

Copy this prompt into an AI assistant when developing a Learning Journey brief for your own teaching context.

---

I want help drafting a commissioning brief for a PRISM Learning Journey.

**My subject / topic / teaching challenge:**
[Describe the decision, dilemma or judgement students struggle with. Start from your context—not from a named pedagogical pattern.]

**Who the learners are:**
[Level, discipline mix, what they already know]

**Time available:**
[Learning time and calendar span, if known]

Please help me clarify, in order:

1. **What students need to become capable of judging or deciding** — the intended capability in plain language.
2. **What evidence would be relevant** — documents, data, cases, accounts or artefacts they should work with.
3. **Where uncertainty or competing interpretations arise** — incomplete information, conflicting findings, contested causal claims, or values in tension.
4. **Whose perspectives matter** — stakeholders affected by the decision and how their interests should enter the learning design.
5. **What makes a recommendation defensible** — standards of reasoning, honesty about limits, and how trade-offs should be handled.
6. **How learners could practise and demonstrate that judgement** — progressive encounters with evidence, comparison, reconsideration, written justification, or other evidence of learning.

Use the exemplar Learning Journey “The Decision That Went Wrong” only as an illustration of one possible design logic:

- Teaching need: reaching a justified institutional recommendation when evidence is incomplete, conflicting and consequential.
- Sequence idea: promising early evidence → careful reading → later conflicting evidence → stakeholder perspectives → committee recommendation, with reconsideration along the way.
- Transferable idea: progressive disclosure of evidence plus justified choice under genuine ambiguity; the fictional AI student-support scenario is not the point to copy.

Do **not** assume I want the same institutional AI scenario, the same product mix, or the same stage titles. Propose a brief that fits **my** decision-making context. If progressive evidence disclosure and recommendation-under-uncertainty fit, say why; if another structure fits better, prefer that.

Return a structured commissioning brief I can paste into PRISM (purpose/focus, learners, learning time, duration, constraints), without inventing validation evidence or claiming the exemplar’s original brief as mine.
