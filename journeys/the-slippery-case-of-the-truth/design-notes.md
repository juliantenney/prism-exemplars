## Retrospective commentary

- **Teaching need (lecturer-facing):** philosophy learners often treat theories of truth as labels to recall rather than instruments for judging contested claims when evidence and testimony conflict.
- **Why this design addresses that need:** the journey forces an early case judgement, then supplies philosophical tools against a living case, then changes the evidentiary picture so reconsideration is unavoidable.
- **Transferable principle:** reuse case-first judgement with delayed theory and forced revision; do not assume the forensic narrative or these exact product types fit every subject.
- **Adaptation cautions:** keep genuine ambiguity if the domain needs it; avoid collapsing competing theories into a single “correct” school; plan how journaled reasoning is carried forward when the case changes.
