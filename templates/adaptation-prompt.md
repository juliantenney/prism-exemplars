# Adaptation consultation prompt — {{title}}

Copy this prompt into an AI assistant when drafting a Learning Journey brief for a related teaching need. Adapt the bracketed placeholders to your context.

---

I want to commission a Learning Journey similar in pedagogical shape to the exemplar “{{title}}”.

**My teaching need:** [describe the problem or requirement for your learners]

**Intended learner capability:** [what should learners become better able to do?]

**Context:** [discipline, level, cohort size, delivery mode, time available]

The exemplar demonstrates roughly this design:

- **Primary pattern:** {{primary_pattern}}
- **Supporting patterns:** {{supporting_patterns}}
- **Transferable principle:** {{transferable_principle}}
- **Learning sequence (exemplar):**
{{learning_sequence_bullets}}

Please help me draft a commissioning brief that:

1. Keeps the transferable principle, not a copy of the exemplar’s subject matter.
2. Names a clear teaching need and intended capability in my context.
3. Proposes an ordered sequence of constituent learning products appropriate to my duration and level.
4. Notes what I should change versus what I should preserve from the exemplar’s design logic.
5. Does not invent validation evidence or claim the exemplar’s original brief as mine.

Return a structured brief I can use to commission the journey in PRISM.
